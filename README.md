# Task 2: Data Visualization and Storytelling

## Objective
Create an interactive dashboard to visualize sales data and present meaningful business insights using Power BI.

## Dataset
- Superstore.csv

## Tool Used
- Microsoft Power BI Desktop

## Visualizations Created
- Sales by Category (Bar Chart)
- Profit by Region (Column Chart)
- Sales by Segment (Pie Chart)
- Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart)
- Total Sales (Card)
- Total Profit (Card)

## Business Insights
- Technology is one of the top-performing categories.
- The West region generates high profit.
- The Consumer segment contributes a large share of sales.
- Sales trends vary over different months.

## Files Included
- Superstore.csv
- Task2_Data_Visualization.pbix
- dashboard_screenshot.png (or Dashboard_Report.pdf)
- README.md

## Conclusion
This dashboard helps analyze sales performance, profit, customer segments, and monthly trends to support business decision-making.
